# saxatur – Terms of Service

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

saxatur ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool run by its owner on their own computer. It cuts short
video clips from the owner's own streams and videos and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram
accounts.

## Use
- The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.
- Only content that the owner has the right to publish is uploaded. The owner reviews every clip and caption
  before it is scheduled.
- Uploads follow TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Content Sharing Guidelines.

## Accounts
- Connecting a TikTok account happens through TikTok's official login (Login Kit). Access can be revoked at any
  time in TikTok under Settings › Security › Apps and services, or by disconnecting in the app.
