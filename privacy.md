# saxatur – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

saxatur ("the app") is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It has no servers, no user
accounts of its own and no analytics or advertising.

## What data the app handles
- **TikTok login tokens** received through TikTok Login Kit after the owner approves access.
- **Basic profile info** (display name and open ID) to show which account is connected.
- **Video and post data** for videos the app uploads: post status and public statistics (views, likes, comments,
  shares).

## How it is used and stored
- Data is used only to upload the owner's clips and to show their statistics to the owner.
- Everything is stored locally on the owner's computer. Tokens are kept in a private file outside the project
  folder and are never logged.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or profiling.
- Videos are sent only to TikTok (and, when chosen, Instagram) through their official APIs.

## Retention and deletion
- Disconnecting the account in the app deletes the stored tokens.
- Access can also be revoked in TikTok under Settings › Security › Apps and services.
- Local statistics are kept until the owner deletes them.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.
